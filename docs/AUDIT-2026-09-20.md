# Nomi — full audit, 2026-09-20

## Summary

Walked every page as owner, as staff, as a brand-new tenant and signed out: 39 GET
routes, 534 screenshots, three languages, 1280px and 390px, plus a re-check of the
September site review and every promise in the docs. The craft inside a page is
real — nothing overflows a phone, no raw keys, RTL mirrors properly, no console
errors. What is missing is the ring around the product. **Four P0s:** the privacy
page names Anthropic while production sends every message to DeepSeek; the Chinese
policy names no processor at all and puts the data on a host that was left months
ago; the deletion page promises in writing what the database is built never to do;
and there is no billing of any kind. Below that: a sales assistant can sign the
go-live attestations, Results has no door, past fifty conversations a waiting buyer
drops out of "Needs you", and a refusal arrives in the success colour. Thirty-two
findings: 4 P0 · 15 P1 · 13 P2. Nothing here blocks the current pilot; the P0s
block charging a stranger.

## Open questions for the founder

1. **Who is the processor?** Is DeepSeek the long-term choice, or Anthropic once
   credits are back? The privacy page has to name one and the answer changes what
   it says about where buyer data goes.
2. **What is the price of Nomi**, and who pays — per workspace, per assistant, per
   message? Nothing can be built until that sentence exists.
3. **Is a sales assistant trusted with money?** Today they can change a price but
   not the payment terms. One rule, either way.
4. **Is outreach (writing first) a feature you are selling yet?** It is four clicks
   deep, half-linked, and its pages are the least finished.
5. **Which channel does a new customer start on?** The first-run page assumes
   WhatsApp; your own live workspace proved Instagram and Messenger are easier.
