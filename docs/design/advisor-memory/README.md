# The advisor's memory: the page and Settings

These shots were rendered 2026-10-07 in a real browser (Brave, which is Chromium; device scale 2). They come from the app's own pages on a local instance with the demo workspace.

**What reached nothing outside:**
- **The model was scripted.** It routes "how many customers", "who has gone quiet" and "how much did we sell" to their entries. It words a short list of facts as they are, and leaves a long list to be shown as the facts themselves, as the product shows a sentence that failed its check.
- **The history was sealed** with a throwaway key made on this machine.
- **No real model and no production data** were used.

Each language and view used a member of staff made for that shot. So the opt-in card could be seen fresh each time, and nothing had to be deleted.

## Files

All five languages (`en`, `zh`, `ar`, `es`, `fr`), desktop (1280 px) and phone (390 px), full page:

- **`card-…`** — after the first answer, the opt-in card (D6). Two buttons of equal weight; the provider named as the privacy page names it; a door to what is kept and for how long.
- **`conversation-…`** — after "Allow", three things:
  - the conversation kept and shown;
  - "New conversation";
  - "Earlier conversations", each with its own delete (asked once).
- **`history-staff-…`** — Settings → The advisor's history, for a member of staff: their own switch (the opt-in sentence, word for word), what it is now, their own download.
- **`history-owner-…`** — the same page for the owner:
  - the workspace's switch (D1);
  - the owner's own conversations;
  - the team, each person with a control to delete their history whole and unread (D2). The control is there for every person, whether they kept anything or not.
- **`your-data-…-en`** — Settings → Your data. The advisor's section has the owner's own history as a file, and a door to the page above.
- **`setup-…-en`** — Setup's new row, "The advisor's history", with whether it is on.

## Checked by script, on every shot

- The card shows after an answer, and is gone after "Allow".
- Two turns are kept in the conversation, and the earlier one is listed.
- On the owner's page, the member of staff is listed.
- Arabic is right to left.
- Nothing is wider than the screen.
- There are no script errors.
