# Home — the three data states

Taken 2026-10-05 on a local instance (Chromium, device scale 2), at 1280 px (desktop) and 390 px (phone), in en, zh, ar, es and fr.

- `home-busy-*`: someone waits (a reply to review), the assistant handled six conversations today, and one date is on the schedule.
- `home-quiet-*`: nobody waits. The card is one calm line; the schedule and the wins carry the page.
- `home-new-*`: a brand-new workspace with nothing yet. It shows the greeting, the calm line, the setup steps, how dates arrive and the way forward.
- `*-grey`: the same page in greyscale. Needs-you keeps its solid dot (●), chores keep their ring (○), and the assistant keeps its outlined face.

The greeting follows the workspace's own hour, so the demo (Asia/Shanghai) says good evening and the new workspace (America/New_York) says good morning.
